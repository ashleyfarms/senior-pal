About Senior Pal

Senior Pal helps people 50 and over find discounts at restaurants, stores, travel companies and services, with the age needed and any rules, in large easy-to-read text.

What you can do

  • Browse discounts by category, like dining, groceries or travel.
  • See the minimum age for each deal.
  • Save the ones you use most.
  • Large text and simple buttons.

Discounts change often, so always ask at the counter before you order.

Free, supported by ads

Senior Pal is free to use. There is nothing to buy and no subscription. We keep it free by showing a small number of Google ads. Your entries are stored on your own device unless a feature clearly says otherwise.

Who makes it

Senior Pal is made by Albert Jones as part of Help-Pal, a family of simple, free apps for everyday jobs. We keep them plain and easy to use. See the whole collection at help-pal-apps.com.
